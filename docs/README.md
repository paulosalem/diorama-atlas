## Historical evidence

[Design decisions](history/design-decisions.md) records the requirements behind
the present standard. [Collection realism](collection-realism.md) and the
[interior rebuild](history/interior-rebuild.md) describe earlier implementation
passes, not a fresh assessment of today's rendering.

Large captures and review reports live in ignored `outputs/`; they are not
required by a fresh checkout. Reproduce them using the development guide.
Historical failed visual reviews remain failures, not evidence of photorealism.

# Swahili Coastal Verandah

**Scene:** `swahili-verandah` · **Region:** Swahili coast, Tanzania

[Visit the verandah](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=swahili-verandah&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

Introduce a coastal architecture shaped by shade, thresholds and Indian Ocean
connections. The trading-house exterior is a coherent fictional building,
not a surveyed house or a claim to represent every Swahili community.

## Spatial and sensory direction

Pale plaster, carved timber, columns, deep verandahs and screened openings
mediate between bright coastal light and shaded living space. Railings,
brackets and turned elements need real depth, supported construction and
side-view legibility.

Preserve the layered approach from street to recessed entrance and the building's
relationship to waterfront context. Timber grain, maintained plaster and
selective weathering should differ by exposure; a uniform decay treatment would
erase the distinction between care and climate.

## View sequence

`arrival` → `entrance` → `balconies` → `coastal`.

## Preserve and extend

Treat shade as a working architectural feature, not simply a dark effect.
Avoid unsupported symbolic interpretations of door ornament or generalized
"tropical" additions unrelated to the referenced building vocabulary.

# Bahian Neighborhood Square

**Scene:** `bahian-neighborhood-square` · **Region:** Bahia, Brazil

[Visit the praça](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=bahian-neighborhood-square&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

An everyday praça explores neighborhood sociability through a sloping shared
space, thresholds and a modest chapel. Bahia's intertwined African, Indigenous
and European histories should not be reduced to colorful façades or permanent
festival decoration.

## Spatial and sensory direction

Painted masonry, deep shutters, iron balconies, tiled roofs and cobbles form
an original ensemble. The slope, drainage and level changes should read through
the paving; color belongs on constructed surfaces with returns and thickness.

Vary maintained painted finishes and selectively worn stone without turning
the whole neighborhood into picturesque decay. Doorways and street edges should
suggest continued domestic life. Keep light, planting and small everyday
arrangements calm enough that the square remains legible.

## View sequence

`arrival` → `chapel` → `facades` → `paving`.

## Preserve and extend

Do not reconstruct Pelourinho, a famous church or an identifiable photographed
block. The anonymity is deliberate. Add atmosphere with coherent daily use,
not stereotyped cultural props or claims of a documented local event.

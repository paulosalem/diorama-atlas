# Central Asian Madrasa Courtyard

**Scene:** `central-asian-madrasa` · **Region:** Uzbekistan

[Visit the courtyard](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=central-asian-madrasa&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

Explore an outdoor room associated with learning and exchange, organized by
openness, sheltered circulation and skilled brick/ceramic construction.
This is a fictional ensemble, not a reconstruction of a named madrasa.

## Spatial and sensory direction

Pointed arcades and shaded galleries wrap the court. Geometric glazed borders
organize larger fields of brickwork; recessed timber doors introduce a different
scale and finish. Ornament should follow architectural structure rather than
float as a flat decorative layer.

Maintain real arcade openings, layered arch profiles, believable wall thickness
and depth into the galleries. Glaze, brick, timber and stone need distinct
responses under strong daylight and cool shade. Small evidence of use must not
overwhelm the ordered, contemplative composition.

## View sequence

`arrival` → `arcade` → `ceramics` → `gallery`.

## Preserve and extend

Use nonfigurative, supported ornament; never present invented inscriptions as
authentic text. Regional photographs guide construction and material
relationships, not an identifiable courtyard plan or an unverified ritual.

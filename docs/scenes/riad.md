# Marrakech Riad Courtyard

**Scene:** `riad` · **Region:** Marrakech, Morocco

[Visit the courtyard](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=riad&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

Explore the home turned inward: shade, water, privacy, skilled surfaces and
tea service organize an architecture of welcome. This is an imagined domestic
courtyard, not a survey of a particular riad.

## Spatial and sensory direction

The fountain anchors citrus planters, low seating, carved plaster arches,
zellige and cedar. Thick doorways continue into furnished rooms and an upper
level. The contrast between a bright open court and shaded recesses should make
the adjoining home feel present, not merely suggested.

Preserve the fine carved geometry, credible joinery, actual fountain basin,
irregular water/reflections and restrained patina. Tea service and textiles
suggest use without obscuring the courtyard's calm hierarchy. Recorded fountain
and service sounds are illustrative; music is a contemporary interpretation.

## View sequence

`courtyard` → `fountain` → `tea` → `cedar` → `doorway` → `upstairs`.
Keep all six approved cameras. The doorway and upstairs views are essential
evidence of depth, not optional extras.

## Preserve and extend

This scene establishes the collection's level of care, not a palette or ornament
kit for unrelated cultures. Improve physical relationships and visible detail
without burying the main composition in decorative additions.

The side upper chambers stand over grounded service wings, with thick outer
doorways, workbenches, shelves and storage jars. Their floors and plinths join
the miniature foundation; they are not floating rooms behind a courtyard
facade. Preserve the original courtyard and salon routes, all six cameras and
the intentionally lifted upper section when inspecting the model from behind.

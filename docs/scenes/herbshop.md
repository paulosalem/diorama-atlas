# Hanoi Herb Shop

**Scene:** `herbshop` · **Region:** Hanoi, Vietnam

[Visit the herb shop](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=herbshop&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

Show how practical knowledge can be arranged spatially: drawers, bundles,
balances, jars and paper parcels give sorting and measurement a visible form.
The scene is cultural interpretation, not medical advice or a documented
dispensary.

## Spatial and sensory direction

Dense cabinetry frames a counter and measuring station; a working dispensary
continues beyond a genuine opening. Daylight should pick out paper, brass,
dried plant textures and worn wood while the recesses remain quieter.

Open drawers require actual interiors. Parcels, botanical fragments and
measuring tools should relate to a task. Jar labels and paper imagery need
real content, but decorative botanical plates must not be presented as verified
ingredient identification or prescriptions.

## View sequence

`room` → `scale` → `drawers` → `herbs` → `passage`.

## Preserve and extend

Keep the shop legible rather than uniformly packed. Distinguish natural timber
variation from dirt, and retain calm working concentration. Preserve this
curated entry separately from the [saved Old Quarter variant](herb-shop-in-hanoi-old-quarter-851851.md).

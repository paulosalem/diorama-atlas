# Kyoto Jazz Kissaten

**Scene:** `kissaten` · **Region:** Kyoto, Japan

[Visit the listening room](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=kissaten&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

Invite attention to coffee and listening as practices that shape a room.
A jazz-oriented kissaten offers a particular interpretation of urban leisure;
the scene does not imply all Japanese cafés follow the same customs.

## Spatial and sensory direction

Siphon brewing, records, speakers, upholstered seating, cups and small tables
share a warm timber interior. A furnished preparation room continues behind
the bar. Window light, quieter booths and restrained practical lights should
support lingering rather than theatrical spectacle.

Glass brewers, ceramic rims, wood grain, cloth and actual record/print imagery
need distinct material response. Coffee traces, service utensils and a turning
record suggest ordinary occupation. Music remains a licensed contemporary
accompaniment, not an archival recording of this invented café.

## View sequence

`room` → `coffee` → `records` → `booth` → `passage`.

## Preserve and extend

Keep visual and auditory calm. Retain the public listening space versus
working preparation-room relationship. Distinguish this curated scene from
the separately saved [1960s interpretation](1960s-kyoto-kissaten-interior-7d985e.md).

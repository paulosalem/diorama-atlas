## Cultural intention

A compact material study of an anonymous neighborhood tavern: patterned tile,
timber touched by service, small plates and glassware make ordinary hospitality
visible. The setting is interpretive, not a documented business.

## Spatial and sensory direction

The counter, shared dining area and bottle/ceramic storage give the room a clear
social and working structure. Preserve its connected service space and the
contrast between immediate tabletop detail and deeper room context.

Natural differences in wood and ceramic finish should remain legible even where
surfaces are clean. Food, bread, cloth and utensils suggest use; repeated identical
clutter does not. Keep real printed labels, plausible glazing and restrained
reflections rather than making every object equally shiny.

## View sequence

`room` → `table` → `bar` → `tiles` → `passage`.

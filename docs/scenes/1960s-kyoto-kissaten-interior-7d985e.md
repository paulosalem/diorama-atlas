## Cultural intention

This retained saved interpretation emphasizes a mid-twentieth-century café's
small rituals: careful brewing, booths, record listening and dessert service.
The period is a design premise, not proof that every object is a verified
1960s artifact.

## Spatial and sensory direction

Warm timber, intimate upholstered seating, a coffee counter and record storage
form the café-family interior. The preparation-room passage keeps the public
room connected to work. Paper menus, cups and serving surfaces should carry
small evidence of repeated use, with fine grain and distinct glass/ceramic
response rather than uniform aging.

Keep the atmosphere unhurried. Do not replace licensed contemporary music
with an unsupported claim of a period recording, or introduce conspicuously
modern branding into the period-oriented visual intent.

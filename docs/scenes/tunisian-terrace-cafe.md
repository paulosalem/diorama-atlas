# Tunisian Terrace Café

**Scene:** `tunisian-terrace-cafe` · **Region:** Tunisian coast

[Visit the terrace](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=tunisian-terrace-cafe&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

Explore a café as an outdoor room: tea, seating, shade and the coastal outlook
invite a shared pause. This is an anonymous authored terrace, not a replica
of a real business or a shorthand for all Tunisian architecture.

## Spatial and sensory direction

Whitewashed levels, blue ironwork, built-in seating, woven/textile surfaces
and tea tables form a calm sequence toward the sea. Steps and retaining edges
must have physical depth and plausible connections between levels.

Give maintained plaster subtle variation rather than blanket cracks. Resolve
tea service, seat construction and appropriate printed detail at close range.
The coastal water should have a different scale and character from sheltered
canals; avoid giant regular waves or reflections unrelated to the scene.

The miniature keeps a continuous ground section beneath the terraces and coast,
with metric mineral grain, roughness and irregular fine relief rather than
repeating horizontal ripples. Trim its sea to the actual wet shoreline: water
must not extend through the exposed geological face beneath dry inland ground.
Keep the original full-scale sea and outlook unchanged.

## View sequence

`arrival` → `tea` → `steps` → `outlook`.

## Preserve and extend

Keep the balance of bright architecture and restful shade. Add tasteful signs
of occupation rather than crowding every table. Do not use a fountain recording
as purported sea ambience or invent a named café's history.

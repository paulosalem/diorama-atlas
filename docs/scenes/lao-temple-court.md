# Lao Temple Court

**Scene:** `lao-temple-court` · **Region:** Northern Laos

[Visit the court](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=lao-temple-court&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

Offer a calm architectural encounter with a northern Lao-inspired precinct:
layered roofs, sheltered thresholds and a shared court. The composition is
fictional and does not document a specific monastery, community or ceremony.

## Spatial and sensory direction

Roof layers, a portico and a separate pavilion frame the court at multiple
scales. Eaves, supports, timberwork, steps and openings should remain legible
from the side as well as the arrival view.

Use light and shade to clarify structure; ornament must not become an opaque
surface hiding construction. Keep maintained materials varied, planting
restrained and the open court spacious. Gentle environmental movement and
illustrative ambience should support quiet attention, not simulate an invented
religious event.

## View sequence

`arrival` → `roof` → `portico` → `pavilion`.

## Preserve and extend

Do not fabricate sacred inscriptions, meanings, ritual objects or monastic
endorsement. Verify any added cultural explanation against appropriate sources.
Avoid turning the scene into a collection of generic Southeast Asian motifs.

Miniature trees retain whole rooted trunk, branch and foliage assemblies.
The distant entrance gate remains in the landscape, but its overhanging roof
and ornaments must not survive alone when the miniature crop excludes its
supports. Keep the portico, pavilion and open working court intact.

# Balinese Subak Terraces

**Scene:** `balinese-subak-terraces` · **Region:** Bali, Indonesia

[Visit the terraces](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=balinese-subak-terraces&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

Make the cultivated hillside legible as work and cooperation, not merely
scenery. Subak involves social and religious institutions as well as irrigation;
the visible water landscape can introduce that context without claiming to
reproduce a real farm, community or hydraulic network.

## Spatial and sensory direction

Terrace contours, narrow bunds, rice, channels and a field shelter create a
walking-scale sequence within a larger valley. Levels should follow plausible
terrain rather than form identical ornamental steps.

Resolve curved rice blades and varied clumps close to the camera. Moist soil,
water edges, exposed ground and maintained plants should meet coherently.
Keep movement gentle and varied; avoid an entire hillside swaying in sync or
repeating the same vegetation stamp. Water should feel sheltered and bounded.

## View sequence

`arrival` → `rice` → `contours` → `shelter`.

## Preserve and extend

Keep agricultural function visible and the composition spacious. Do not assign
an invented sluice an authentic operational history, or reduce a cooperative
tradition to a picturesque background.

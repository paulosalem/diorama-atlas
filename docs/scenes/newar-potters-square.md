# Newar Potters' Square

**Scene:** `newar-potters-square` · **Region:** Kathmandu Valley, Nepal

[Visit the square](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=newar-potters-square&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

Show pottery production as part of neighborhood space: drying clay, working
thresholds and shared circulation belong together. This is an invented
Newar-inspired quarter, not a copy of a photographed square.

## Spatial and sensory direction

Brick façades, carved timber screens, deep eaves, stepped passages and varied
earthenware frame an intimate court. Open vessel mouths, differing profiles and
restrained drying tones should read as a process rather than a decorative
display of identical pots.

Preserve recessed latticework, believable roof support, actual doorway depth and
clear routes between work areas. Light reveals brick and clay without making
every surface equally clean or equally damaged. Sparse workshop/urban ambience
is illustrative, not a field recording of this fictional quarter.

## View sequence

`arrival` → `pottery` → `windows` → `shrine`.

## Preserve and extend

Keep the making-and-neighborhood relationship primary. Add culturally supported
working detail rather than generic market clutter. Do not invent meanings for
religious elements or copy a recognizable monument to increase specificity.

The miniature crop includes the full depth of both side buildings and the
ground beneath them. Do not reduce those buildings to thin frontage strips
to tighten the overview. The drying court, shrine route, original cameras
and separation between the two supported sections remain unchanged.

# Istanbul Meyhane

**Scene:** `meyhane` · **Region:** Istanbul, Türkiye

[Visit the meyhane](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=meyhane&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

Explore shared-table sociability: small dishes, service and conversation
organize an unhurried evening. This is an anonymous tavern interpretation,
not a named establishment or a universal account of Istanbul dining.

## Spatial and sensory direction

The table is the social center, with meze, glasses, bread and serving materials.
The bar, tiled surfaces, bottles and reflected light extend the room; a real
service space explains the work behind hospitality.

Preserve believable food/plate scale, transparent glass, actual bottle-label
imagery and the difference between maintained timber and localized handling
wear. Partly cleared settings should imply a sequence of service, not random
mess. Warm practical light and subdued room/service recordings support the
table without implying recorded conversations from an actual meyhane.

## View sequence

`room` → `table` → `bar` → `tiles` → `passage`.

## Preserve and extend

Keep the focus on companionship and the physical table. Avoid a generalized
"old tavern" theme that erases differences from the Lisbon variants sharing
the same implementation family.

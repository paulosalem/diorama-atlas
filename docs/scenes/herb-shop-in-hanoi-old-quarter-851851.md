## Cultural intention

The saved brief emphasizes the compressed organization of a narrow urban shop:
many small containers, repeated measuring tasks and visible working memory.
It is an anonymous interpretation, not a record of a particular Old Quarter
business or advice about herbal treatment.

## Spatial and sensory direction

Cabinetry, hanging bundles, jars and a balance counter establish density without
closing the circulation or dispensary sightline. Filtered daylight should
distinguish the front working area from the deeper room.

Resolve drawer cavities, paper folds, jar rims and brass details at close range.
Give neighboring wooden pieces independent variation, avoiding an identical
pattern across the entire cabinet wall. Sparse paper-handling sound and small
botanical fragments support work rather than a theatrical apothecary fantasy.

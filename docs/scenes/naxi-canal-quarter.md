# Naxi Canal Quarter

**Scene:** `naxi-canal-quarter` · **Region:** Yunnan, China

[Visit the canal quarter](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=naxi-canal-quarter&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

Show water as part of neighborhood organization: crossings, street edges,
timber frontages and cultivated thresholds form a connected setting.
The quarter is informed by Naxi-associated old-town architecture, not a copy
of a named bridge or a claim about all Naxi life.

## Spatial and sensory direction

A stone arch bridge, separate balustrade elements, retaining stones, lattice
frontages and layered tiled eaves frame the canal. The waterway must remain
open beneath the crossing and spatially continuous with its banks.

Reflected façades, damp edges and restrained waterline light should share an
irregular flow rather than repeated sine stripes. Give timber and paving
piece-level variation; plants soften edges without hiding construction.
Check benches and other small structures for actual support and ground contact.

## View sequence

`arrival` → `bridge` → `stream` → `lattice`.

## Preserve and extend

Keep canal, bridge and buildings one coherent neighborhood. Do not substitute
generic decorative water, copy a photographed plan or imply an authentic local
wildlife recording from the illustrative sound palette.

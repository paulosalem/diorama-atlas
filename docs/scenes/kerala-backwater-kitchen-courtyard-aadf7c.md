# Kerala Backwater Kitchen Courtyard

**Scene:** `kerala-backwater-kitchen-courtyard-aadf7c` · **Region:** Kerala, India

[Visit the kitchen](https://paulosalem.github.io/diorama-atlas/?scene=kerala-backwater-kitchen-courtyard-aadf7c&inspect=1)

## Cultural intention

Connect food preparation to domestic architecture and a cultivated waterside
setting. Brass vessels, spice preparation and coconut-working implements
suggest everyday knowledge, not a single definitive model of a Kerala home.

## Spatial and sensory direction

A semi-open kitchen combines timber, terracotta, plaster, cooking vessels and
a recessed hearth. The pantry and deeper garden/backwater context must remain
actual connected spaces. The transition between working shade and outdoor
greenery is central to the experience.

Model hearth mouths as cavities, with fuel, restrained embers and steam.
Vary brass finish and soot where heat explains them rather than darkening
everything. Preserve the single coherent physical canal and its water motion;
do not add an overlapping decorative water plane.

## View sequence

`room` → `hearth` → `spices` → `verandah` → `passage`.

## Preserve and extend

Preparation materials should indicate real actions and plausible reach.
Keep the sound palette illustrative and the architectural ventilation legible.
Avoid assigning the invented home a real address, community or ritual history.

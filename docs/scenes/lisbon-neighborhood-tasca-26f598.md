## Cultural intention

This saved variant emphasizes the familiar rhythms of neighborhood lunch:
small plates, a working counter, modest tables and objects handled repeatedly.
It is not a recreation of a named Lisbon restaurant.

## Spatial and sensory direction

Azulejo surfaces, timber, glass and ceramic service frame a compact social room.
Linen, bread, utensils and partly cleared dishes should make lunch visible
without implying a frozen crowd that is absent from the scene. A furnished
service passage explains the room's practical support.

Let daylight and warm practical light distinguish table, bar and deeper work
areas. Preserve ceramic ornament while varying its finish and neighboring
materials. Do not replace patterned tile with random dirt or repetitive
algorithmic discoloration.

## View sequence

`room` → `table` → `bar` → `tiles` → `passage`.
